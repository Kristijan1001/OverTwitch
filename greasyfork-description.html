OverTwitch — Cinematic Chat Overlay for Twitch

OverTwitch floats Twitch chat on top of the video player. With your mouse away it is just message text over the stream — fully transparent, outlined so it stays readable, nothing boxed in and nothing to burn into an OLED. Move your mouse over it and the real chat comes back solid, with the input, badges, viewer cards, mod actions and emote tooltips. It is Twitch's own chat, not a copy, so everything works the way it normally does — including sending messages.

✨ Features

  • Invisible when idle, full chat on hover — transparent background with a configurable text outline while you watch; solid, interactive chat the moment you point at it.
  • Real Twitch chat — usernames, badges, viewer cards, mod actions, replies and message sending all behave natively.
  • Anchored to the player — placement is stored as a percentage of the video, so it keeps its position and proportions in theater and fullscreen.
  • Drag & resize — drag the top bar to move it, pull any edge or corner to size it.
  • Live-preview settings — background colour and opacity, font family (including a custom one), size, colour, outline colour, weight, hide usernames, hide timestamps. Every change previews instantly.
  • Global settings — one set for every channel and VOD, so anything you change applies everywhere. No per-channel overrides drifting out of sync.
  • Opens by itself — the on/off state is remembered across reloads and browser sessions. Leave it on once and it is simply there.
  • Auto-claim channel points — optional, clicks the bonus chest for you.
  • Live streams and VODs.

🎛️ Controls

  • 💬 button in the player controls → show / hide the overlay.
  • Alt + C → same, from the keyboard.
  • Hover the overlay → solid chat with the input, drag bar, resize grips and ⚙ settings.

🎨 Defaults

Out of the box: fully transparent with outlined text, timestamps hidden, auto-claim on. All of it is adjustable in the ⚙ panel, and hiding usernames or timestamps applies while hovering too, not just when idle.

ℹ️ Notes

  • Works on twitch.tv channel pages and VODs. Requires Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey.
  • Collapse Twitch's chat sidebar for the best result — the video goes full width and chat floats on top of it. The script never touches Twitch's collapse button or fights your layout.
  • Live chat is embedded from Twitch's own popout chat page, which is what makes it lay out and scroll correctly over the player. The trade-off is one extra chat connection.
  • Third-party emote add-ons (7TV, BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ) show up in the overlay as long as they also run on twitch.tv/popout/*/chat — most of them do by default.
